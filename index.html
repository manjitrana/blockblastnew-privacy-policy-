Privacy Policy

Block Blast New

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AutonomousOne ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Block Blast New mobile game ("the App"). It explains the information handled by the App and by third-party services used to support advertising.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or postal address to play the core game.

Gameplay and preferences

Game state and preferences such as scores, saved game progress, achievements, sound and music settings, vibration settings, animation settings, and theme preference are stored locally on your device using Android app storage. This locally stored gameplay information is not sent to AutonomousOne servers by the game-storage code.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Rewarded ads may be offered for in-game features such as reviving or receiving bonus continues.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information through the advertising SDK to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and support advertising operations. Depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and the services enabled, this may include:

  • Device identifiers, including an advertising ID where available;
  • IP address and approximate/general location inferred from it;
  • Device, operating system, language, and app information;
  • Ad impressions, interactions, diagnostics, and performance data.

These practices are controlled in part by Google and may vary according to your settings and location. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Internet Access and App Permissions

The Android app manifest declares internet access and network-state access to support network connectivity and advertising, and vibration access for gameplay feedback. The manifest we reviewed does not declare permission to access contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location. Advertising SDKs may use network access to request and display ads.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your gameplay progress or local game preferences. Information may be processed by Google AdMob as described above when advertising is displayed. We do not operate a game account system or a gameplay server in the version reviewed. If the App's features or third-party SDKs change, this section and the policy will be updated as needed.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general puzzle game and does not intentionally ask children to submit personal information directly to us. Third-party advertising services may process device or usage information as described in their own policies. The App's target audience and advertising configuration should be reviewed in the store console and AdMob settings before release, particularly if the App is directed to children or may appeal to children.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset the game's data, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to Android backup settings. Advertising providers may retain information under their own policies and controls; please consult Google's privacy resources for details on their retention and available choices.

7. Security

Locally stored game data is kept within the App's Android storage area. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You should keep your device and operating system up to date.

8. Your Choices

You can manage available advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings. Availability of choices, such as ad personalization or advertising ID controls, depends on your device, region, and current Google settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App, its features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
